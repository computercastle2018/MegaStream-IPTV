# MegaStream

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Users and Purpose

Arabic-first viewers browse and play their configured live channels, movies, and series using TV remotes, keyboards, or pointers.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Android, LG webOS, macOS, and Windows share subscription, licensing, appearance, and playback workflows. Preserve existing accounts, licenses, playback history, filters, favorites, and quality controls. Preserve Classic, Modern, and Studio as selectable designs.

## Current Approved Work

Add a separate TV Pro template on all platforms. The user selected IBPlayer Pro's catalog and playback arrangement as the reference. Use MegaStream branding and existing content artwork, not another application's logos or bundled assets. Remote focus, Arabic RTL, Back navigation, and accessible controls must remain functional.

## Evidence

The authorized Kings Pro test device provides the live visual reference. Catalog and playback success require checks on real configured content; a successful build alone is not verification.
